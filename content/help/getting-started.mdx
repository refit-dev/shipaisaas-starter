Welcome. This guide takes you from a fresh account to your first AI response in a couple of minutes.

## Create your account

Sign up with an email address and password, or with Google. If you use email, we send a six-digit code to verify the address — enter it and you land in onboarding, where you tell us your business name and a few details used across the app.

A workspace is created for you automatically. Everything you do — conversations, task runs, usage — belongs to that workspace.

## The dashboard

The sidebar has four places worth knowing:

- **Dashboard** — activity over the last 30 days, plus how many requests you have used this month.
- **Tasks** — one-shot templates for common jobs. Fill in a form, get a result.
- **Usage** — daily and monthly request counts against your plan limits.
- **Billing** — your current plan, and the Stripe portal for changing or cancelling it.

## Your first request

Click the sparkle button in the bottom-right corner to open the assistant. Type a question and press Enter. The response streams in as it is generated, and the conversation is saved — reopen the assistant later and your history is there.

The model picker sits in the chat toolbar. Switching models mid-conversation is fine; the next message simply uses the model you picked.

## What counts against your plan

One chat message is one request. One task run is one request. Your plan sets both a daily and a monthly cap, and you can see both on the Usage page. If you hit a cap, the app tells you which one — nothing is silently dropped.
